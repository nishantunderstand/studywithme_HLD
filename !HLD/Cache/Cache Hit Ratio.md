"Cache Hit Ratio is the percentage of requests served from cache without querying the database.  
  
Cache Hit — data found in cache, served instantly from memory.  
Cache Miss — data not in cache, falls through to database, result stored in cache for future requests.  
  
Calculation: Cache Hits divided by Total Requests × 100.  
Example — 90 cache hits out of 100 requests = 90% Cache Hit Ratio.  
  
Higher ratio means lower database load, lower latency, higher throughput.  
  
In production, a Cache Hit Ratio below 80% usually means cache size is too small, eviction policy is wrong, or the data being cached is not frequently reused.  
  
Cache Hit Ratio is the primary metric to evaluate whether your caching layer is actually working."
