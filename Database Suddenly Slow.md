If Your Database Suddenly Becomes Slow, **Don’t Just Add An Index.** 🚨  
  
Most developers jump straight to indexing, but that’s only one possible cause.  
  
In this video, I cover the complete checklist I use to troubleshoot slow databases:  
  
✅ Check slow queries (EXPLAIN, slow query logs)  
✅ Database connections & connection pooling  
✅ Locks & deadlocks  
✅ CPU, memory & disk bottlenecks  
✅ Traffic spikes and sudden load  
✅ Database growth & partitioning  
  
The goal is to **find the actual bottleneck** instead of guessing.
